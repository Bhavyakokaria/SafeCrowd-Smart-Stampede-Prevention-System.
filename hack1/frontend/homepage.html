About Us

We are a team of innovators passionate about building safer spaces for large gatherings. Our project, Smart Stampede Prevention System, combines Artificial Intelligence, IoT, and real-time monitoring to detect and prevent dangerous crowd situations before they turn into disasters. Our solution uses smart sensors, live data analysis, and AI-powered alerts to monitor crowd density, predict risks, and assist authorities in managing events more effectively. By blending cutting-edge technology with human-centered design, we aim to save lives, improve crowd management, and make public gatherings safer for everyone. This project is not just about technology—it’s about responsibility, safety, and innovation. Through this hackathon, we’re showcasing how AI and creativity can solve real-world problems and create a lasting social impact.
